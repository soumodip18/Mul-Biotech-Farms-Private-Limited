Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you provide?
We provide agriculture, aquaculture, biotechnology, environmental consultancy, GIS and sustainability services. Our service portfolio covers land solutions, controlled farming, aquaculture, environmental assessment, project documentation (DPR), GIS mapping, biotechnology solutions and training support.
Do you prepare project reports?
Yes. We prepare Detailed Project Reports (DPRs), feasibility studies, technical documents and cost estimations for government projects, CSR initiatives, and private sector investments.
Do you provide farm setup support?
Yes. We support setup and planning for hydroponics, aquaponics, mushroom cultivation, aquaculture systems and other farming systems. We provide technical planning, site assessment and implementation guidance.
Do you provide training?
Yes. We conduct farmer training programs, institutional workshops, student training programs and field demonstrations. Training covers specific technologies as well as sustainable farming practices.
What is our delivery model?
We follow a structured six-step process: requirement discussion, site assessment, technical planning, proposal & documentation, implementation support, and training & monitoring. This ensures comprehensive support from planning through completion and beyond.
