About Mul Biotech Farms

Mul Biotech Farms Private Limited is an environmental intelligence, biotechnology, and integrated agricultural solutions company.

We combine biological science, environmental engineering, GIS, remote sensing, digital agriculture, AI, IoT, and monitoring systems to design, implement, monitor, and improve agricultural, aquatic, ecological, and climate-resilient systems.

Our work ranges from hydroponics, aquaponics, aquaculture, agroforestry, mushroom biotechnology, regenerative agriculture, and environmental biotechnology to GIS platforms, Digital MRV, climate intelligence, carbon projects, natural capital, and institutional technical advisory.

The objective is simple:

«Connect biological systems with scientific and digital intelligence to create measurable, resilient, and scalable environmental and agricultural systems.»
