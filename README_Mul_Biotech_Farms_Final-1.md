## About Mul Biotech Farms

**Mul Biotech Farms Private Limited** is an Environmental Intelligence and Biotechnology company developing integrated solutions for agriculture, ecosystems, natural capital, climate resilience, and digital agriculture.

We combine **biological science, biotechnology, environmental engineering, GIS, remote sensing, AI, IoT, digital platforms, and monitoring systems** to design, implement, monitor, and improve biological, agricultural, aquatic, ecological, and environmental systems.

Our work ranges from **hydroponics, aquaponics, aquaculture, agroforestry, mushroom biotechnology, regenerative agriculture, and environmental biotechnology** to **GIS, remote sensing, digital MRV, climate intelligence, carbon and natural-capital projects, and institutional technical advisory**.

> **Our objective is to connect biological systems with scientific and digital intelligence to create measurable, resilient, and scalable environmental and agricultural systems.**
