Agri Times

Online Agricultural Publication | 2025
Organic Coverage Online
"The Kalimpong district administration is explicitly leveraging aquaponics as a structural defense against the region's prominent water scarcity woes... There are currently about 1,500 registered fishery farmers in the district. Out of those, regional officials expect over 1,000 farmers to rapidly transition and adopt aquaponics setups in the near future. The 100-square-foot closed-loop ecosystems use zero synthetic fertilizers. Nutrient-rich water feeds crops like spinach, cabbage, bok choi, and lettuce, while filtering water for indigenous fish (Trout for high-altitude systems and Mahseer for low-to-mid elevation zones)."

Vertical Farming Daily

International Controlled Environment Agriculture Journal | 2025
Organic Coverage International
"Kalimpong initiative as a premier template for 'controlled environment agriculture' (CEA) in volatile terrain. By combining the market value of organic leafy greens (like lettuce, bok choi, and spinach) with fresh local fish yields, a 100-square-foot unit acts as a micro-enterprise. This protects small landholders and homestay owners against crop failures caused by changing mountain weather patterns."
